01

Monday

Ingredients

  • ½ cup natural yogurt
  • ½ cup almond milk
  • 1 apple
  • Cinnamon powder

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients together
  2. Refrigerate overnight

Marinade

  • 6 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 6 tbsp fresh orange juice (~1 whole orange)
  • ¾ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼–½ tsp onion powder (optional)
  • Pinch of pepper or chili flakes (optional)

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken in orange juice mixture
  2. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 22–25 minutes
  3. Cover with foil for the first 15 minutes to keep juicy
  4. Optional: broil last 2–3 min for a golden glaze

Ingredients

  • 1 can tuna in water
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 lime
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • Garlic powder, salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Drain tuna and mix with onion, lime juice, garlic powder, salt & pepper
  2. Halve the avocado and remove the pit
  3. Fill each avocado half with the tuna mix
  4. Serve cold — no cooking needed

Tuesday

  • 3 tbsp chia seeds
  • Cinnamon powder
  • Vanilla
  • Splenda (1 packet)

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients together

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 1 egg
  • Oatmeal
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Mix egg with oatmeal, salt, pepper & garlic powder
  2. Dip chicken in the mixture
  3. Air fry at 375°F (190°C) for 12–15 min, flipping halfway
  4. Check that internal temp reaches 165°F (74°C)

Suggested Sides

Mushrooms + ¾ cup potatoes in the air fryer with rosemary, olive oil & garlic powder (~15 min)

Ingredients

  • Wrap

  • Bell pepper, spinach, mushrooms
  • Leftover crispy chicken from lunch

Steps

  1. Assemble wrap with sauce, veggies & leftover chicken
  2. Heat in a small oven or pan with lid

Wednesday

  • Stevia

Ingredients

  • Flank steak
  • Bell pepper
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Potatoes
  • Paprika, salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Cut meat into large cubes; season with paprika, salt & pepper
  2. Layer in Instant Pot: onion rounds → diced tomato → meat → pepper strips
  3. Add ~2 cups water
  4. Cook on high pressure for 30 minutes

Steps

  1. Place eggs in a pot and cover with cold water
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 10–12 min
  3. Transfer to an ice bath, cool, then peel
04

Thursday

Ingredients

  • 6 oz steak
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Garlic powder
  • Soy sauce & oil

Steps

  1. Toss steak strips with soy sauce, garlic powder & oil — marinate 15+ min
  2. Air fry steak at 400°F (200°C) for 6–8 min, shaking halfway
  3. Add sliced onions & tomatoes, air fry at 380°F for 3–4 min
  4. Toss everything together and serve (Peruvian lomo saltado style)
